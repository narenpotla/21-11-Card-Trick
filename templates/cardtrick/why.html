Why Does It Work?

It's actually pretty simple.

The trick doesn't guess your card. It slowly narrows down where your card can be until there's only one place left.

🎴 Before we start

Your card could be anywhere.

⬇️ Round 1

You tell me which pile has your card. That pile is always placed in the middle. Now your card can only be somewhere here.

Nice! We've already ruled out most of the deck.

⬇️ Round 2

Do the exact same thing again. The possible places get even smaller.

Now there are only a few places left.

⬇️ Round 3

One last time. Only one place is left.

🃏
11th

That's where your card always ends up.

✨ That's the secret!

It doesn't matter which pile you choose. The important part is that the chosen pile is always placed in the middle. Every round, your card has fewer possible places to hide. After three rounds… there's only one place left.

🤓 Curious how the math works?

Splitting 21 cards into 3 piles and asking which one your card is in narrows things down by a factor of 3 — from 21 possibilities to just 7. Every round does that same ÷3 narrowing again.

3³ = 27  ≥  21

That's why it takes exactly 3 rounds, not 2: three rounds of narrowing by 3 gives 3³ = 27 possible outcomes, which is just enough to cover all 21 cards. Two rounds would only give 3² = 9 — not enough to tell every card apart.

And because the pile you choose is always tucked into the exact middle of the reassembled deck, the only card that can survive being "in the middle" three rounds in a row is the exact center of a 21-card stack — the 11th card.
