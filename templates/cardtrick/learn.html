Learn the Trick

Try It with a Real Deck! ⭐

Grab any 21 cards and follow these simple steps.

  1. Step 1

    Deal the Cards

    Deal all 21 cards into 3 piles. One card at a time.

  2. Step 2

    Ask Your Friend

    Ask them, “Which pile is your card in?” Don't ask what the card is.

  3. ↓

    Step 3

    Put That Pile in the Middle

    Pick up all three piles. Always place the chosen pile in the middle.

  4. ↻ × 3

    Step 4

    Do It Again

    Repeat the same steps 2 more times. That's 3 rounds in total.

  5. 11

    Step 5

    Reveal the Card

    Count down to the 11th card. 🎉 That's their card!

Now go fool your friends. 😉
